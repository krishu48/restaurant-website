Michelin Starred Award Winning
✦ SINCE 1985

Our Heritage

Founded in 1985, La Belle Vie has been a cornerstone of fine dining excellence for over three decades.

What started as a small family-run bistro has grown into one of the most celebrated restaurants in the region, known for its commitment to quality, innovation, and exceptional hospitality.

Our philosophy is simple: use the finest ingredients, prepare them with passion and precision, and serve them with warmth and elegance.

Chef Antonio Rossi

Executive Chef & Co-Owner

His philosophy is rooted in respecting traditional techniques while embracing modern innovation. He personally curates each menu, ensuring every dish tells a story.
